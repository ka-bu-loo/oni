Conditions & calculation notes

Waterweed data updated for U59: no Bleach Stone; 20 kg Salt Water per domestic plant per cycle. Update notes. Other calculation assumptions are listed below.

Think of this as a starting plan for a settled colony. Keep your plants comfortable and leave some breathing room for travel, spoilage and the occasional missed harvest. The safety surplus helps with that.

    We work backward from the meal and keep enough eggs to replace your breeders. Counts round up, but supplies use the exact estimate underneath. Wild applies to plants, not your food-producing ranches. Smoker fuel is included; fertilizer ingredients can be expanded. Power, cooling, cooking time and Gas Range fuel are not. Resource endpoints still need a supply of their own.
